# TripPilot AI — Travel FAQ

## 1. Flight Booking
TripPilot AI searches available flight offers using the connected flight API. Search results may include flight routes, departure times, arrival times, airlines, and prices.

A flight search result is not a confirmed airline ticket. A booking record created in the demo application does not mean a real airline reservation has been issued.

## 2. Baggage Allowance
Baggage allowance depends on the airline, route, cabin class, and fare conditions.

Passengers should check cabin baggage limits, checked baggage allowance, prohibited items, and excess baggage charges using the airline's official information.

## 3. Travel Documents
Required travel documents depend on the destination, nationality, and purpose of travel.

Passengers may need a valid passport, a visa or other entry authorization, and additional documents depending on their circumstances. Verify requirements with official government sources before travelling.

## 4. Flight Cancellation and Refunds
Cancellation eligibility and refund amounts depend on the airline, fare conditions, booking channel, and applicable rules.

Passengers should review their ticket conditions and contact the airline or booking provider for confirmation.

Cancelling a demo booking in TripPilot AI only changes the application's demo booking record. It does not cancel an actual airline reservation or initiate a real refund.

## 5. Flight Changes
Flight changes may be subject to airline rules, fare conditions, availability, and additional charges.

Check the conditions of the specific booking before making changes.

## 6. Check-in
Online check-in availability and opening times vary by airline, airport, route, and passenger circumstances.

Passengers should consult their airline's official website for check-in times and boarding requirements.

## 7. Travel Assistance
TripPilot AI helps users understand travel information and compare available flight offers.

If the knowledge base does not contain enough information to answer a question, the assistant should say so rather than inventing a policy.
